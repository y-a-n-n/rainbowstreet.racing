Here is a set of optimized text prompts designed for image generation models (like Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, or DALL-E).

Since you are building a 2D side-scroller, these prompts are heavily engineered to output flat, orthographic side profiles with solid backgrounds, making it easy to cut out the transparent PNGs and integrate them directly into your local Phaser project.

### 1. Parallax Background Assets

*Note: Include your background reference image with these.*

2D game asset, seamless horizontally tiling background, distant synthwave city skyline at night. Deep purple and dark blue sky, retro glowing grid, distant neon skyscrapers. Strict flat 2D side-scrolling perspective, no foreground elements, crisp vector art style.

**Top Layer (Foreground Road)**
