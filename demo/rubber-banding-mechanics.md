# Rubber-banding mechanics

This demo shows the catch-up slice:

- the player trailing by more than 600px receives 1.1x acceleration and top speed
- the trailing player's passive boost refill progress is shown beside their boost row
- paused refill progress resumes when that same player falls behind again
